On-demand snow removal in Duluth, MN

DRIFT is an app that connects homeowners with independent local plow operators — no contract needed. Map your property, name your price, track your plow live, and pay only when it snows. Serving Duluth, Hermantown, Cloquet, Esko, Proctor and Superior.

What you can book

Driveway plowing, sidewalk clearing, car dig-outs, and roadside jump-starts.

How it works

Outline your driveway on a satellite map, set a snow-depth trigger for automatic plowing (or book on demand), and a nearby independent operator accepts the job and clears it. No contracts, no hidden fees, before & after photos on every job.
