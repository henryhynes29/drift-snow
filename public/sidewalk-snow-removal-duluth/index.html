Local know-how

The 24-hour rule

In Duluth, property owners have to clear snow and ice from the sidewalk within 24 hours of the end of a snowfall, across the full width of the walk on every side of the property. Corner lots also have to clear the curb cuts down to the street.

If a walk isn't cleared, the city can send a compliance letter, then have a contractor clear it and bill the property owner. You also can't shovel or blow the snow into the street or alley.

Superior has a similar rule: clear the sidewalk in front of your property within 24 hours of a snowfall.

FAQ

Questions people ask

How long do I have to clear my sidewalk in Duluth?

24 hours after the snowfall ends. If more snow falls, the city pushes re-checks back to 24 hours after the latest snowfall ends.

What happens if I don't clear it?

The city can send a compliance letter. If it still isn't cleared, a contractor can clear it and the cost is charged to the property owner.

Can someone clear my sidewalk and driveway in one trip?

Yes. Add the sidewalk when you book and the same driver handles both.
