Local know-how

Plowing past the bridge

Park Point is a long, narrow sandbar, and every trip on or off it goes across the Aerial Lift Bridge. During the shipping season the bridge lifts for boats, which can add a few minutes for a driver coming from the mainland. The live map shows you exactly where yours is.

Wind moves snow around out here. Mark where you want it piled, away from where it'll blow back across the driveway.

Sand and soft edges. Pin the edges of your driveway, any landscaping and anything low to the ground so the blade stays on the pavement.
