FAQ

Booking a plow

How do I book a snow plow with DRIFT?

Open driftplowing.com, map your driveway on the satellite map once (outline where to plow and where the snow goes), and you'll get a recommended price. When it snows, tap once to send your request to local drivers. You'll see when one accepts and can watch them head over.

Do I need to download an app?

No app store needed. DRIFT runs in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen so it opens like a regular app and can send you notifications.

How fast can a plow get to me?

Your request goes to drivers nearby right away. How fast depends on how many drivers are out and how busy the storm is. Offering a bit more than the recommended price usually gets you picked up first.

Can I schedule a plow before a storm?

Yes. Pick a day and a 2-hour window up to 6 days ahead. Drivers add scheduled jobs to their routes the night before, and they have to check in before the window starts.

Do I have to be home?

No. Your map shows the driver exactly what to plow, where to put the snow and what to avoid. You'll get before and after photos when it's done.

What happens if no driver takes my request?

Your request stays open for 20 minutes. If nobody accepts, it expires and the hold on your card is released. You aren't charged.

Can I book for a parent or a second property?

Yes. Add each address as its own property in the app and book whichever one needs plowing.

FAQ

Prices and fees

How is my price set?

When you outline your driveway, we measure the area and recommend a price based on its size, the slope and how much snow fell. You choose what to offer. Most two-car driveways in Duluth come in around $58–$64 per storm. See the full price guide.

What fees are there?

Your offer, plus a $10 call-out fee that goes straight to the driver and a $5 DRIFT booking fee. That's it. No subscription, no monthly fee.

Why does deeper snow cost more?

It's a bigger, slower job. The recommended price goes up about 15% for 4–8 inches, 30% for 8–12 inches and 50% for a foot or more.

Can I offer less than the recommended price?

Yes, down to $10. Lower offers can take longer to get picked up, especially in a big storm.

Are there surge prices?

No. The price you offer is the price. You decide whether to offer more to get picked up faster.

Do I tip the driver?

Tipping is optional and 100% of every tip goes to the driver.

FAQ

Payments and cancellations

When is my card charged?

When you book, your card is held for the amount, not charged. It's charged only after the driver finishes the job.

How can I pay?

Credit or debit card, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Payments are handled by Stripe and DRIFT never sees your full card number.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel before a driver arrives at no charge. After a driver has arrived or started, you may be charged.

What if I'm not happy with the job?

Report it in the app within 48 hours with photos. Every job also has before and after photos, which help sort things out quickly.

FAQ

Your driveway, hazards and damage

Why do I have to mark hazards?

Drivers can't see what's under the snow. Marking curbs, raised concrete, well caps, septic covers, landscaping and anything else a plow could hit is the best way to avoid damage. You only do it once.

Where does the snow go?

Wherever you mark on your map. In Duluth it's against city rules to push snow into the street or alley, and Minnesota law doesn't allow snow on public roads, so mark a spot on your own property.

What if something gets damaged?

Report it in the app right away, within 48 hours, with photos. Drivers are independent businesses, and anything you didn't mark on your map is at your own risk, which is why marking hazards matters.

My driveway is steep. Is that okay?

Yes. Mark how steep it is when you set up and the recommended price goes up a little so drivers want the job.

Do you plow gravel driveways?

Yes. Mark the surface as gravel so drivers know before they accept.

FAQ

Your driver

Who does the plowing?

Independent local plow operators who use DRIFT to find jobs. They're not DRIFT employees. DRIFT is the app that connects you, handles booking and payment, and gives you live tracking and photos.

What do drivers need to sign up?

A photo of their driver's license, their vehicle registration, the gear they use, and a signed independent contractor agreement. DRIFT doesn't provide insurance for drivers.

Can I get the same driver every time?

Yes. Tap the heart on your receipt to save a driver as a favorite. When you book and they're online, they get your job first for 2 minutes.

Can I talk to my driver?

Yes. You can message or call your driver in the app once they accept.

Duluth & Superior

Local snow rules

How long do I have to clear my sidewalk in Duluth?

24 hours after the snow stops, across the full width of the walk. Corner lots also have to clear the curb cuts. Read the full rules.

What's the sidewalk rule in Superior, WI?

Superior's ordinance gives property owners 24 hours after a snowfall to clear the sidewalk in front of their property.

When is alternate side parking changeover in Duluth?

Every Sunday between 4 and 8 p.m. Here's how winter parking and snow emergencies work.

Can I push snow into the street?

No. Duluth doesn't allow snow in the street or alley, and Minnesota law prohibits putting snow on public roads.

Is there overnight parking in Cloquet in winter?

Not on city streets or in alleys from midnight to 6 a.m., November 1 through March 31, according to local reporting on the city's rule.

For plow operators

Driving for DRIFT

How do I start driving for DRIFT?

Go to the plow jobs page or tap "Drive with us." You can see open jobs and what they pay before you fill anything out.

How much do drivers make?

80% of every offer, plus the full $10 call-out fee and 100% of tips. On a typical two-car driveway that's around $49 per job before tips. See the math.

How do I get paid?

Through Stripe, straight to your bank. You can set up payouts as yourself or as your business.

Do I need my own insurance?

Yes. DRIFT doesn't insure drivers, and many personal auto policies exclude plowing for pay. Read about plow insurance.

Can I choose which jobs I take?

Always. You see the pay, the distance, the size and the property map before you accept, and you can set a home zone so you only get alerts for jobs near you.

Can I drop a job I scheduled?

Free until 3 hours before the window. After that it counts as a late cancel on your record. During a live job you can report a problem and give it back before you start plowing.

I run a plow company with several trucks. Can we join?

Yes. Each driver gets their own login and picks their own jobs, and you can get paid as your business.
