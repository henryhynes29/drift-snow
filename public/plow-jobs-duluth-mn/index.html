What the work is

Homeowners map their driveway, mark hazards and where the snow goes, and post a job when it snows. You see the pay, the distance, the size and the property map before you accept. Plow it, snap before and after photos, and you're paid through Stripe.

What it pays

You get 80% of the customer's offer, the full $10 call-out fee and every tip. On a typical two-car driveway that's around $49 before tips. See the per-storm math.

What you need

  • A truck with a plow (or a snowblower for sidewalk jobs)
  • A driver's license and vehicle registration
  • Your own insurance that covers plowing for pay. Read about plow insurance
  • A smartphone

How it works for drivers

  • Look first: see open jobs and pay before any paperwork.
  • Home zone: only get alerts for jobs near you.
  • Storm mode: build a route the night before a storm.
  • Regulars: customers who favorite you get you first.
  • Problems: car in the driveway? Report it and give the job back with no penalty before you start.
  • Taxes: miles on every job are logged for you.

Running a plow company?

Fill the gaps between contract stops. Each driver gets their own login, and you can get paid as your business.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is DRIFT hiring snow plow drivers in Duluth?

DRIFT works with independent plow operators, not employees. Sign up, see open jobs, and take the ones you want.

Do I need my own plow?

Yes, for driveway jobs. Sidewalk jobs can be done with a snowblower.

Can I plow for DRIFT as a side job?

Yes. Go online when it snows and you're free, and offline whenever you want.
