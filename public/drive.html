Good to know

  • You work for yourself. Drivers are independent contractors, not DRIFT employees. You decide when you're online and which jobs you take.
  • You bring the gear. Your own truck, plow, fuel and tools.
  • Insurance is on you. DRIFT doesn't insure drivers, and most personal auto policies don't cover plowing for pay. Check with your agent, or compare snow-plowing business coverage at NEXT Insurance.
  • Taxes. You'll get a 1099 through Stripe. The app logs your miles on jobs so tax time is easier.
