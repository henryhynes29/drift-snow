Good to know

  • You work for yourself. Drivers are independent contractors, not DRIFT employees. You decide when you're online and which jobs you take.
  • You bring the gear. Your own truck, plow, fuel and tools.
  • Insurance is on you. DRIFT doesn't insure drivers. Check with your insurance agent that you're covered for plowing for pay.
  • Taxes. You'll get a 1099. Payouts go to your bank through Stripe.
