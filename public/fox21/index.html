The honest part

Who actually does the work

DRIFT is the app, not the plow company. The people who clear your driveway are independent local operators — Duluth folks with a plow who pick up jobs through DRIFT. You see each driver's rating and truck, track them live, and get before-and-after photos on your receipt.

The 15% off is our discount, not theirs. It comes out of DRIFT's booking fee, so your driver still earns their full pay on your job. You pay less; they don't.
