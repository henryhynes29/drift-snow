Which side this week

The rule: park on the side the calendar shows for that week (odd or even house numbers). Weeks run Monday to Sunday, and you switch sides on Sunday between 4 and 8 p.m., the only time both sides are allowed.

Snow emergencies

The city can declare a snow emergency by 4 p.m. on any day except Sunday, and it takes effect at 8 p.m. that day. Move your car to the plowed side of the street between 4 and 8 p.m. When the emergency ends, move it back to the scheduled side by 8 p.m. The city says residents no longer need to move cars to amnesty lots. To get alerts, sign up for Northland Alert.

Tickets and towing

An alternate side ticket is $36 and a snow emergency ticket is $42, and your car can also be towed. If it was towed, call the city at (218) 730-5400.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What is the alternate side parking schedule in Duluth?

Duluth uses an odd/even calendar. Each week (Monday to Sunday) you park on either the odd-numbered or even-numbered side of the street, and the sides switch every week. The full 2026–27 schedule is in the table on this page. Posted street signs take priority.

When is alternate side parking changeover in Duluth?

Every Sunday between 4 p.m. and 8 p.m. Both sides are allowed only during that window.

What do I do in a Duluth snow emergency?

Move your car to the plowed side of the street between 4 and 8 p.m. on the day it's declared, then back to the scheduled side by 8 p.m. after it ends. Sign up for Northland Alert to get notified.

How much is an alternate side parking ticket in Duluth?

$36. A snow emergency ticket is $42, and your car can be towed.

What number do I call if my car was towed in Duluth?

The city lists (218) 730-5400.
